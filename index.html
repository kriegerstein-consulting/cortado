Questions

Before you request

Is this real mentoring or just a paid favor?

It is compensated time — and that is the point. Bankers can be generous with their calendar without feeling exploited, and you skip the guessing game of whether it is too much to ask for free.

What if a banker cancels?

Your slot is released and you can request another open slot with the same banker or someone else.

Do bankers need approval from their firm?

Every current banker confirms compliance with their employer's outside-activity policy before a profile goes live. Sessions are limited to career and recruiting conversations — never confidential deal, client or trade information.

Why don't profiles show names?

Banker profiles show role, employer, location and experience — what the person offers. A banker's name and contact details are shared only once they confirm your request. That protects bankers' privacy and keeps the focus on the experience you are booking. Bankers see the name of the student who requests a chat.

What does "former" mean on a profile?

Former bankers share first-hand experience from past roles, for example three years as an analyst in New York. They are labelled clearly, so you always know whether you are talking to someone who works there today.

Is this only for investment banking?

Cortado starts with banking, where the information gap in recruiting is especially large. The same model applies to consulting, law and tech.
