Questions

Before you book

Is this real mentoring or just a paid favor?

It's compensated time — and that's the point. Bankers can be generous with their calendar without feeling exploited, and you skip the guessing game of "is this too much to ask for free?"

What if a banker cancels on me?

You're refunded automatically and can rebook any other open slot, with priority access to the same banker's next available time.

Do bankers need approval from their firm?

Every banker confirms compliance with their employer's outside-activity policy before a profile goes live. Sessions are scoped to career and recruiting conversations only — never confidential deal, client, or trade information.

Is this only for investment banking?

The Coffee Desk started here because the information gap in banking recruiting is especially sharp, but the same model works for consulting, law, and tech — those are next.
